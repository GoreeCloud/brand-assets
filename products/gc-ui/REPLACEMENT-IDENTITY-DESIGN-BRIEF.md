## Diagnosis — what failed in Frame Relay

Source inspection and founder rejection support these findings:

- The new mark simply illustrated three literal material concepts using **three offset, rounded colored frame paths**, rather than an ownable, instantly recognizable symbol.
- The dark rounded-square app tile and violet/teal/white treatment made the icon look like generic developer-tool branding; this is a design assessment, **not** a verified trademark or market-uniqueness claim.
- The wordmarks relied on generic system text without a demonstrable custom wordmark rhythm, letter-shaping, or optical relationship to the symbol.
- The interface artwork was a formulaic block-and-label composition explaining Cloud Canvas, Surface and Lens, rather than original, emotionally compelling product artwork.
- Integrity checks confirmed source safety, not design merit. Passing SVG metadata parsing, hash tests and graphic-color contrast checks cannot imply the founder considers the visual result premium.
- The project incorrectly moved to a fully scripted family of six assets before testing **one strong core symbol** with the founder.

These are qualitative observations and process corrections, not newly approved colors, icon dimensions, typography, or layout tokens.

## Mandatory direction for the next concept

1. **Design the idea before drawing the icon.** Define a concise brand thesis, what GoreeCloud UI uniquely represents, its relationship to the umbrella, and why its visual identity should be recognizable without words.
2. **Do not recycle the rejected frame motif.** Do not use three nested/offset open-window paths, small blocks standing in for Canvas/Surface/Lens, a generic cube, a generic shield, an orbit of gradient blobs, borrowed symbols, or decorative effects that merely simulate premium quality.
3. **Commit to a distinctive silhouette.** Evaluate the primary mark in one color first, with its shape and negative space clear at favicon sizes. Color should be purposeful and subordinate to the symbol. No new compulsory GoreeCloud palette is authorized by this brief.
4. **Design real typography.** A wordmark needs deliberate proportions, tracking, optical alignment and credible standalone typography. A default system-font label next to a symbol is not enough for final approval.
5. **Treat browser favicon, launcher and artwork as different design problems.** A 16px favicon may require a specifically simplified optical master; a launcher tile needs balanced shape and masking; artwork needs conceptual substance beyond diagrammatic UI rectangles.
6. **Keep every other GoreeCloud product distinct.** Check GoreeShield (privacy), Wardveil (cybersecurity), GoreeCloud Mesh (integration), and the permanent umbrella mark for possible confusion, without redefining their identities.
7. **Use verified GoreeCloud UI foundations.** Honor existing approved design language and platform accessibility requirements. Sophistication must survive light/dark contexts, high zoom, reduced motion, contrast, and various device sizes.
8. **No images in chat.** All discussion, planning and approvals can begin as text; original visual proposals should be inspected only through an explicitly authorized separate design workflow when appropriate.

## Text-only visual-identity discovery — November 15, 2026

**These are competing design hypotheses, not artworks, specifications, approvals, color choices, or permission to generate images in this chat.** Their purpose is to give a future visual designer a concrete set of alternatives and failure criteria **before** producing another full asset suite. No option is a winner without a real rendered review.

The product's functional meaning is **the interaction/design language connecting GoreeCloud experiences**, not cloud storage, privacy enforcement, malware defense, or networking. The new symbol must represent this discipline without borrowing the umbrella circular-G, a window-control node, or imagery from GoreeShield/Wardveil/Mesh.

**No route is selected or ranked.** The next visual exploration should test no more than **one coherent core-symbol/wordmark pair at a time** and stop if it fails the first objective critique; do not automatically render six variants, manufacture ICO/PNG derivatives, or migrate consumers.

### First visual-review protocol (future, outside this chat)

1. Write a *one-paragraph visual thesis* for the selected route, including why the symbol belongs only to GoreeCloud UI, what it deliberately **does not** portray, and its distinctness from the permanent GoreeCloud circular-G and existing product marks.
2. Inspect the actual primary mark **in monochrome** and at **16, 24, 32, 64 and 128 pixels**, without a decorative tile or product name. Record whether counters, silhouettes and optical weight remain clear; visual evidence must be viewed, not inferred from vector metadata.
3. Inspect the wordmark separately on light and dark reading surfaces. Assess custom letterform construction, kerning, proportions, the relative size of `UI`, and whether the icon and wordmark look designed together.
4. Compare at least one credible competing direction using the same review conditions. Reject imitational, generic or too-complex results before discussing colors, animations, mascots, art and packaging.
5. Record a founder decision **for the exact visual revision**: `accept`, `revise` or `reject`. Silence or generic "continue" language is **not approval**. Document observed defects and corrections. Do not alter approved masters during review.
6. Only after a positive visual decision: inspect artwork as its own original composition and verify accessible contrast, real favicon/browser behavior, native launchers and platform derivatives with source hashes and rollback.

## Enforced rejected-asset source boundary — November 15, 2026

The preserved Frame Relay archive is explicitly excluded from promotion but **not exempt from provenance and integrity checks**. The central brand repository now runs `scripts/verify-rejected-identity-boundaries.mjs` in its existing `npm test`/CI workflow. It independently reads the original archive's founder-rejection decision and six pinned Git blob hashes and checks *all actual graphic files under `products/` and `brand/`* for **exact byte-for-byte matches** outside that archive. This prevents accidental reuse of an unmodified rejected SVG as another brand's icon. The test reads files only; it does not render or generate images.

The UI consumer separately runs `tests/rejected-identity-adoption.test.js` under its regular Node test suite, checking all active `assets/` image bytes against the same six published rejected blob fingerprints, direct rejected-archive links in `lab/` and `src/` runtime files, and existing package asset boundaries. These two repositories' checks are complementary and **not proof of visual quality, original authorship, or immunity to edited derivatives**. Changed/derived shapes, similar motifs, and unauthorized remote asset references still require professional visual review and governance.

The only authorized continuation after this rejection is a **different concept** with a new identifier, its own source provenance and explicit founder approval before any official promotion. The legacy Aperture Weave source pins and GoreeCloud umbrella circular-G remain unchanged.
